02 / Research directions

Questions I want to answer.

Work in progress: research ideas I’m developing around retrieval, evaluation, and robust learning systems.

Research direction

Beyond text-only RAG

How can retrieval preserve the structure of long tables, visual documents, and video? I’m interested in extraction, representation, and evaluation that keep evidence usable across modalities.

Research direction

Agents that earn their complexity

When does agent orchestration improve retrieval and reasoning? I want to measure the tradeoffs between answer quality, coordination cost, latency, and reliable tool use.

Early-stage concept

The Fragility Horizon

Stress-test neural resource allocators under increasing noise, delay, and supply shocks. The question: when does performance degrade gradually, and when does it collapse abruptly?

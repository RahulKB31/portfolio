Research

The questions that interest me most usually start where a demo stops working: a table spans twenty pages, an agent takes the wrong step, or a model meets data it has never seen before.

Tables, images, and video in retrieval

A number means little without its row, column, unit, or footnote. I’m exploring how retrieval can keep that context intact across long tables, scanned documents, images, and video—and how to test whether the answer really follows from the evidence.

When do agents actually help?

More agents can mean more ways to get stuck. I want to find out when splitting a task actually improves the result, when a simpler approach works better, and how retrieved noise changes an agent’s decisions and tool use.

The Fragility Horizon

How much pressure can a learned decision system take? My early idea is to increase noise, delays, and supply shocks gradually, then map where a neural resource allocator starts to fail. I want to distinguish a slow decline from a sudden collapse, and look for warning signs before either happens.

These are research directions in progress, rather than published findings.
